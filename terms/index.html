What the app does

KitchenScan gives a visual estimate of the condition of kitchen items from one photo. It’s a visual check, not a lab test, and not professional, medical or safety advice.

Results can be wrong. Use your own judgment, follow the maker’s instructions, and when in doubt, replace the item.

Your photos

Only check items you own or are allowed to photograph, and avoid photos that show people or personal information.

Account

KitchenScan Pro

Checks are part of KitchenScan Pro, an auto-renewing subscription billed to your Apple ID: yearly or monthly, at the price shown before you buy. Pro includes up to 10 checks a day.

The yearly plan can start with a free trial of 3 days (one per Apple ID), with up to 2 checks a day. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, the yearly price is charged when it ends.

A subscription renews unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Manage or cancel it in your Apple ID settings. Deleting the app or your account doesn’t cancel it. Refunds are handled by Apple.

A photo the app can’t check (too blurry, or not a kitchen item) doesn’t count, up to 10 a day. If your plan ends, your saved checks stay in Kitchen; new checks need a plan again.
