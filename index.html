Questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then KitchenScan Pro. You can also get there from KitchenScan: Settings › Manage subscription.

Turn off auto-renew at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Deleting the app or your account doesn’t cancel the subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are made and refunded by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I have a new phone. How do I get my plan back?

Sign in to the App Store with the same Apple ID, open KitchenScan and tap Settings › Restore purchases. If you signed in to KitchenScan with Apple or Google, sign in with the same account.

How many checks can I do?

Up to 10 a day with KitchenScan Pro, and up to 2 a day during the 3-day free trial. A photo the app can’t check (too blurry, or not a kitchen item) doesn’t count, up to 10 a day.

Where are my checks saved?

Only on your phone. Signing in keeps your plan with your account, not your checks, and deleting the app removes them. Photos are sent once to be checked and aren’t kept on our servers.

How do re-check reminders work?

Each saved item gets a next-check date from its last result. Items that come due are bundled into one reminder on Saturday morning, at most one a week. Change the date on the item’s page, or turn reminders off in Settings › Reminders.

How do I delete my account and data?

In KitchenScan, open Settings › Profile › Delete account and data. This removes your account and plan record from our servers and the checks on your phone. It doesn’t cancel an App Store subscription.

Is the result a lab or safety test?

No. KitchenScan gives a visual estimate from one photo. Results can be wrong, so use your own judgment, follow the maker’s instructions, and when in doubt, replace the item.
