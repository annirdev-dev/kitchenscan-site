Privacy Policy

Last updated November 8, 2026

This policy explains what KitchenScan, published by annirdev, does with your information.

The short version

  • Sign in with Apple or Google, or use the app as a guest.
  • Your checks are saved only on this phone.
  • A photo is sent once to be checked and isn’t kept on our servers.
  • We don’t sell your data and the app has no ads.

Who is responsible

annirdev, based in Finland, is responsible for your information (the “controller” under the EU General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR). Contact: annirdev@gmail.com.

Photos you check

When you check an item, the photo is resized on your phone and sent over an encrypted connection to our server, which passes it to Google’s Gemini API, an AI service from Google Cloud. The AI describes the item and any wear it sees, and our own rules turn that into the result.

Our server doesn’t save the photo. Under Google’s terms for paid use, Google doesn’t use it to train or improve its products; it may keep it for a limited time only to detect misuse.

What our server keeps

  • An app ID created by Firebase Authentication. As a guest it’s a random ID, not linked to your name, email or phone number.
  • If you sign in with Apple or Google: the email address and name they share with us (Apple lets you hide your email behind a relay address and choose whether to share your name; Google also shares a link to your profile picture), and when the account was created and last used. We use them only to keep your account and plan, and to answer support requests.
  • Your KitchenScan Pro subscription as Apple reports it: plan, free-trial status, end date and Apple’s transaction ID. Apple handles payment; we never see your card or billing details.
  • How many checks were made today, so the daily limit works. With a subscription it’s counted on the subscription (by Apple’s transaction ID), so the limit is the same on every phone that uses it.
  • A technical log of each check: time, result, how long it took and what it cost. Never the photo or the app ID.
  • Like any web service, Google Cloud’s request logs record the connection’s IP address and device type.

When you contact us

If you email support, we use your email address, your message and the app ID you include only to answer you.

Keeping the service safe

We use Firebase App Check with Apple’s App Attest to confirm that requests come from the real app on a real iPhone. Apple and Google handle the device signals this needs.

On your phone

Photos, results and the answers you add (how often you use an item, when you got it) are stored only on this phone. They can be part of your own iPhone backups.

Delete them any time in Settings › Profile, or by deleting the app.

Re-check reminders are scheduled on this phone. Nothing about them is sent to us, and you can turn them off in Settings.

Why we use it

  • Your consent: you agree before your first check that photos are sent to the AI service. You can withdraw it at any time with Delete account.
  • To provide the app: your account, subscription and daily check count are needed to run KitchenScan Pro.
  • Legitimate interests: App Check and the technical log keep the service safe, working and affordable.

How long we keep it

  • Photos: not stored by us.
  • App ID, sign-in details and subscription link: until you delete your account.
  • Today’s check count for a subscription: replaced every day. It stays after Delete account, under Apple’s transaction ID and without your app ID, so deleting can’t restart the day’s limit.
  • Technical and request logs: up to 30 days.
  • Support emails: as long as needed to answer you.

Where data is processed

Our server runs on Google Cloud (Firebase) in the United States. Google processes the data for us under its data processing terms, which include the EU Standard Contractual Clauses for transfers outside the EU.

Apple handles purchases and Sign in with Apple, and Google handles Google sign-in, under their own privacy policies.

Your choices and rights

Settings › Profile › Delete account removes your checks from this phone, deletes what our server holds for your app ID (including your sign-in details), unlinks your subscription, disconnects Sign in with Apple or Google, and deletes the app ID itself. Nothing is sent after that unless you agree again.

Deleting your account doesn’t cancel a KitchenScan Pro subscription; cancel it in your Apple ID settings.

Under the GDPR you can ask for a copy of what we hold about your app ID, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email annirdev@gmail.com, or use Contact support in Settings, which adds your app ID so we can find your data.

You can also complain to the Office of the Data Protection Ombudsman in Finland, or to the data protection authority where you live.

Children

KitchenScan isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, the app asks you again before your next check. The date at the top shows the current version.
